## Key Differences

### AI Agent Integration
Compliance Intelligence MCP is built for AI agents using the MCP protocol. AI agents call tools like `search_regulations(query="Medical device recall")` or `entity_compliance_check(query="Apple Inc")` directly — no enterprise procurement, no complex API documentation.

Bloomberg and LexisNexis are enterprise legal research platforms designed for human researchers. API access requires enterprise contracts and significant integration effort.

### Data Sources
Compliance Intelligence MCP combines SEC EDGAR filings, FDA enforcement actions, OFAC sanctions, and export control data into a single compliance screening workflow. Bloomberg and LexisNexis cover more jurisdictions but at enterprise price points.

### Pricing Model
Compliance Intelligence MCP: pay per call with no monthly commitment.

At $0.06/call, you get 1,650 compliance checks for the price of one LexisNexis month — without an annual contract.

## When to Choose Each

**Choose Compliance Intelligence MCP when:**
- You're an AI agent doing compliance due diligence
- You need SEC EDGAR filings and FDA enforcement data
- You want sanctions screening without enterprise contracts
- You need pay-per-use without annual commitment
- You're building a compliance screening product

**Choose Bloomberg Compliance when:**
- Your team does manual compliance research
- You need global legal and regulatory coverage
- You have budget for enterprise tools
- You need real-time news alongside structured data

**Choose LexisNexis when:**
- Your team does legal research and litigation support
- You need global court case coverage
- You have existing enterprise contracts
